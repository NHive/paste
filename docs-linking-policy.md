# Product linking policy

- Canonical developer portfolio: https://www.nbhive.com/en/products, /zh-cn/products and /zh-hk/products. nbhive.cn remains a mirror; new external links use www.nbhive.com.
- Portfolio → each product's official website; Paste downloads → ArcRelay's release section. ArcRelay history → archived Paste documentation and portfolio.
- StarScan → Stareceipt only for receipt/expense organization; Stareceipt → StarScan only for general document/PDF scanning. Other works use an About section and the portfolio.
- Use descriptive brand/task anchors in visible, substantive content and ordinary HTTPS anchor links. Keep footer to one other-works entry. This is an editorial limit, not a search-engine threshold.
- Respect destination languages; do not invent locale routes or downloadable platforms. No tracking parameters by default. Editorial nonpaid links need no nofollow; paid links must use sponsored and user-submitted links ugc where applicable.
- No reciprocal-link requirement, keyword stuffing, copied landing pages, or claims that linking guarantees rankings. Each product keeps its own canonical, privacy/terms, rights and support. Stareceipt is not owned by Shenzhen Changning Technology Co., Ltd.
- Review monthly: status/TLS, redirect destination, language, canonical/indexability, links to unavailable releases; inspect Search Console exposure/clicks and referral-to-download performance when access exists. Log actual observations; do not fabricate ranking evidence.
- Rollback: remove inbound links before removing portfolio routes; revert source and publish through existing Jenkins, retaining archived Paste URLs and canonical domain rules.

References: [Google link spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) and [canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls).

Public portfolio and About copy describes product tasks and useful related apps. Ownership and support boundaries are maintenance guidance; do not repeat them as disclaimers in product recommendations. Existing legal pages and copyright notices remain authoritative.
