# Software for everyday work

From collaborating across devices to scanning documents and organizing receipts, these apps help with different parts of everyday work.

## ArcRelay — collaborate across your own devices

ArcRelay continues the work started by Paste. It is an open-source, LAN-first application for moving clipboard content, files and input between trusted devices. Use it when your work spans computers on a network you control.

[Explore ArcRelay and official downloads](https://arcrelay.app/en/products/arcrelay#downloads). Existing Paste users can read the [migration notes](/en/download) and [archived Paste guide](/en/guide/getting-started). Paste is no longer maintained; automatic account, subscription and history transfers have not been confirmed.

## StarScan — turn paper into useful documents

StarScan is a document scanning app for a global audience, with no ads and no watermarks. It focuses on scanning pages, recognizing text and organizing PDF documents. It suits notes, contracts and other everyday paperwork when you want a readable digital copy.

[Explore StarScan document scanning](https://starscanapp.com/). Check its official download section for current platform availability.

## Stareceipt — organize receipts and expenses

Stareceipt focuses on receipts rather than general document scanning. It helps people organize purchase records and expenses, including users in Canada, the United States and other supported markets. Choose it when keeping track of receipts matters more than creating a general-purpose PDF.

[Explore Stareceipt receipt organization](https://www.stareceipt.com/). Its official website provides product details, supported markets and current plans.
